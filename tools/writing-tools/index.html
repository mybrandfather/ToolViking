Common questions

Writing Tools FAQ

Is pasted text uploaded?

No. ToolViking processes text in your browser and does not upload or store it.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time divides word count by a selected words-per-minute rate. It is a planning estimate because readers and subject difficulty vary.

What types of text can I measure?

Use articles, essays, reports, product copy, scripts, speeches, newsletters, and other text-based drafts.
