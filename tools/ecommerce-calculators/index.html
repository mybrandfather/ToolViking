Common questions

Ecommerce Calculators FAQ

What costs should an ecommerce profit calculation include?

Include product cost, marketplace and payment fees, discounts, shipping, packaging, returns, advertising, and applicable taxes or duties.

Are marketplace fee estimates exact?

No. Fee schedules and account rules can change. Verify the current fee structure for your platform and location.

How often should inventory inputs be updated?

Update demand, lead time, and safety-stock assumptions whenever seasonality, suppliers, or sales velocity materially change.
