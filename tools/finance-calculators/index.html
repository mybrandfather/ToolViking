Common questions

Finance Calculators FAQ

Are these accounting calculators?

They are planning tools, not a substitute for accounting, tax, legal, or investment advice. Reconcile important decisions with your records and qualified advisers.

Do ToolViking finance calculators store my numbers?

No. The calculators on this page run in your browser and ToolViking does not receive or store the values you enter.

Which finance calculator should I start with?

Start with the decision you need to make: use margin and markup for pricing, break-even for volume targets, and cash runway or burn rate for cash planning.
