Common questions

Lead & Automation Calculators FAQ

How should automation time savings be valued?

Count hours that can realistically be avoided or reassigned and use a loaded labor cost when appropriate.

Does estimated ROI guarantee savings?

No. Adoption, reliability, maintenance, training, error handling, and demand all affect realized value.

Which lead metric should I start with?

Start with response time and conversion rate, then use lead value or missed-call revenue to estimate the commercial impact.
