Common questions

Marketing Calculators FAQ

Which metrics should be reviewed together?

Pair cost metrics such as CPM and CPC with conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, ROAS, and revenue quality. No single metric describes campaign performance.

Can these tools replace an analytics platform?

No. They calculate from the values you enter; they do not collect campaign events or resolve attribution.

How should I compare campaigns?

Use the same date range, currency, attribution window, audience definition, and conversion event.
