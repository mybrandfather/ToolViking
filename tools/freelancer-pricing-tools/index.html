Common questions

Freelancer Pricing Tools FAQ

Should a freelance rate include non-billable time?

Yes. Sales, administration, learning, time off, and unpaid gaps reduce the hours available to bill clients.

Is hourly pricing always best?

No. Hourly pricing is a useful floor, while fixed or value-based pricing may fit work with clear scope or high business value.

What belongs in project costs?

Include contractors, software, travel, assets, payment fees, and other costs required specifically for the engagement.
