Common questions

Contractor Calculators FAQ

What should a contractor estimate include?

Include labor, payroll burden, materials, subcontractors, equipment, permits, travel, disposal, overhead, profit, and project-specific risk.

Is markup the same as margin?

No. Markup divides profit by cost; margin divides profit by revenue.

Should estimates be checked against completed jobs?

Yes. Comparing estimates with actual labor, materials, overhead, and change orders improves future pricing.
