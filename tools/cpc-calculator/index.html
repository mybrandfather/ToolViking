CPC Calculator

Calculate average advertising cost per click from campaign spend and recorded clicks.

CPC is a campaign metric, not a profitability measure. Check attribution, conversion quality, revenue, and platform billing data before changing spend.

How to use this tool

How to use the CPC Calculator

Enter advertising spend and clicks from the same campaign, date range, currency, and reporting source.

  1. Choose one campaign and reporting period.
  2. Enter total advertising spend and recorded clicks.
  3. Calculate CPC and evaluate it with conversion and revenue metrics.
Method

Formula or calculation method

Cost per click = advertising spend ÷ clicks.

Interpretation

What the result means

Average CPC shows how much you paid for each recorded click. A lower CPC is not necessarily better if traffic quality or conversion value is lower.

Example

Worked example

$2,500 in spend divided by 4,200 clicks equals an average CPC of about $0.60.

Avoid

Common mistakes

Avoid mixing currencies, date ranges, billed spend with estimated spend, all clicks with link clicks, or campaigns with different objectives.

Best use

When to use it

Use it to review paid-search or paid-social efficiency, compare campaigns, and connect click cost with conversion rate and cost per acquisition.

Common questions

CPC Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate CPC?

Divide total advertising spend by the number of clicks recorded for the same campaign and period.

Is a lower CPC always better?

No. Click quality, conversion rate, customer value, and profit determine whether traffic is worthwhile.

Should I use all clicks or link clicks?

Use the click definition tied to your objective and keep it consistent. Platform-reported all-click and link-click totals can differ.
