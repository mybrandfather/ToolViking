Common questions

Business Operations Tools FAQ

What makes an operations estimate useful?

Document the period, unit, owner, and assumptions behind each input, then compare the estimate with actual results.

Can these tools replace workforce or project software?

No. They are fast planning aids rather than systems of record, schedulers, or payroll platforms.

Do I need an account?

No. The tools run in your browser without signup.
