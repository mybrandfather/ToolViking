# ToolViking Product Strategy

Evaluate ideas as products before coding them.

## Decision labels
Classify substantial new ideas as one of:
- **ADD** — strong fit, useful now, clear user/search intent, practical maintenance.
- **IMPROVE FIRST** — promising but needs differentiation, clearer intent, or better scope.
- **SAVE FOR LATER** — useful after ToolViking has more traffic, users, infrastructure, or revenue.
- **SKIP** — weak fit, redundant, thin, costly, risky, misleading, or hard to maintain relative to value.

## Evaluation criteria
Consider:
1. Real problem severity and frequency.
2. Search or discovery intent.
3. Ability to deliver meaningful value without a paid API.
4. Differentiation from generic calculators or commodity AI wrappers.
5. Maintenance burden.
6. Privacy/security implications.
7. Natural internal-link cluster and category fit.
8. Monetization path: ads, affiliate, premium template/dashboard, subscription, qualified lead, or paid product.
9. Whether the idea strengthens ToolViking instead of scattering it.

## Default product direction
Prefer browser-side calculators, generators, planners, estimators, checklists, dashboards, templates, localStorage, copy/download, and export features. Use expensive AI/API infrastructure only when the value clearly justifies recurring cost.

## External ideas
Treat recommendations from other AIs, competitors, marketplaces, or articles as research inputs. Do not implement them automatically. Compare them with the existing ToolViking roadmap and current repository before deciding.

## Implementation gate
Before adding a new public route, confirm it is not a duplicate of an existing catalog item and that the page can contain enough genuine utility/content to stand on its own.
