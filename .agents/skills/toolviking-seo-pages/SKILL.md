# ToolViking SEO Pages

Build pages around usefulness and a distinct search intent, not page count.

## Tool page standard
A strong tool page should normally include:
- Specific descriptive title and meta description.
- One clear H1 matching the primary user task.
- Functional tool near the top.
- Short explanation of what the tool calculates/produces.
- Input/output definitions where ambiguity exists.
- Formula or method when appropriate.
- At least one realistic worked example for calculators where it helps understanding.
- Practical interpretation of the result.
- Limitations/assumptions where relevant.
- Contextual links to genuinely related ToolViking pages.
- FAQs only when they answer real follow-up questions.

## Quality rules
- Do not keyword-stuff headings or repeat the same paragraph across routes.
- Do not fabricate search volume, expert endorsements, customer usage, savings, rankings, or statistics.
- Do not produce dozens of location/industry variants whose only difference is a keyword.
- Do not add FAQ/schema markup that disagrees with visible page content.
- Financial/business calculators should make clear that outputs are estimates where appropriate.

## Internal linking
Create clusters by user problem and category. Prefer a few useful contextual links over indiscriminate sitewide linking.

## Technical consistency
When a route is public and indexable, keep canonical URL, sitemap, page path, and internal links consistent. Do not leave stale sitemap entries after deleting/redirecting routes.
