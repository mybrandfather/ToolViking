How ToolViking works

Calculator methodology

Transparent methods, visible assumptions, local processing, and a documented correction path.

Methods

Formulas and assumptions

Each priority calculator explains its formula, inputs, interpretation, example, and common mistakes. Outputs are planning estimates unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Privacy

Local calculation

Current calculators run in your browser. ToolViking does not receive or store the numbers or text entered into these tools.

Quality

Testing and review

Automated checks cover calculator execution, invalid output, metadata, schema, links, sitemap membership, canonical URLs, and key page structure before release.

Corrections

If a formula, explanation, or result appears wrong, email hello@toolviking.com with the tool URL, inputs, expected result, and a reliable source where applicable. Reproducible errors take priority.

Update policy

Evergreen formulas are reviewed when product changes are made. Rules, fees, taxes, and platform-specific assumptions can change; pages that depend on them should state the limitation and must not be treated as authoritative current policy.

Decision standard: validate material financial, tax, legal, employment, advertising, and operational decisions against source records and qualified professional advice.
