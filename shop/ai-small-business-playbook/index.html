Sample recipe

Write a local outreach email

Problem: cold outreach sounds generic and gets ignored. Outcome: a short, specific email with a relevant observation and one clear ask.

You are helping a small-business owner write a local outreach email.

Business: [YOUR BUSINESS]
Prospect: [COMPANY NAME]
Observation: [REAL OBSERVATION FROM THEIR SITE]
Offer: [YOUR OFFER]
Tone: helpful, direct, not pushy

Write:
1. Three subject lines
2. One email under 120 words
3. One follow-up message
4. A checklist to verify before sending

AI Small Business Playbook FAQ

Is this a generic AI ebook?

No. It is structured as 50 business recipes with prompts, workflows, placeholders, examples, worksheets, review steps, and links to relevant ToolViking tools.

Who is it for?

Entrepreneurs, freelancers, contractors, small agencies, ecommerce sellers, local service businesses, and owner-operators who need practical workflows more than AI theory.

Can I buy it today?

Not yet. Checkout and secure digital delivery are intentionally not connected until the product files, page, SEO, tests, and Stripe implementation plan are verified.

Will the recipes guarantee results?

No. They help organize work and improve execution, but results depend on the offer, market, pricing, follow-up, quality, and human review.
