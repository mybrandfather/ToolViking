# ToolViking AI Small Business Playbook — Product Blueprint

## Positioning
**50 Practical Ways to Use AI to Save Time, Find Customers and Run Your Business**

Planned launch price: **$14.99**

This is an operational playbook, not an AI theory book. Every recipe must contain:
1. The business problem
2. The desired outcome
3. A short human-led workflow
4. A copy/paste AI prompt with editable placeholders
5. A checklist, worksheet, script, or template
6. A relevant ToolViking deep link when an existing tool genuinely helps
7. A verification step so the owner reviews AI output before using it

## Product-led loop
Google/search intent → free ToolViking tool → contextual playbook CTA → relevant recipe → deep link back to ToolViking → adjacent tool/product discovery.

## Editorial guardrails
- Never tell the reader to paste confidential customer, employee, financial, medical, credential, or payment data into a public AI model.
- AI drafts must be reviewed by a human before customer-facing, legal, financial, safety, employment, or contractual use.
- Do not claim AI output is guaranteed accurate.
- Do not fabricate statistics, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Keep recipes practical enough to complete in roughly 5–20 minutes unless clearly labeled as a larger workflow.
